What To Expect At Events

Here’s what to expect during Sephora events.

Participants

Our events typically accommodate 10-30 guests. Guests can RSVP or walk-in and attend. No registration is required. Events can last 1-4 hours. We hope you will join us!

Cost

Our events are FREE to our clients! We encourage you to bookmark as many events as you want so we can remind you 24 hours before to attend.

Value

We hope you learn something new by attending our events. We also provide incentives and free gifts with purchase if you purchase a product at one of our events.
